--- book: Deadly Harvest page: 80 tags: water,water-all,water-distilled,water-mains-potable,water-mineral,water-purified ord: 5 --- A great many of the water treatment plants and distribution networks were built over 100 years ago. Not only have they reached the end of their useful lives, they suffer a chronic lack of investment.
