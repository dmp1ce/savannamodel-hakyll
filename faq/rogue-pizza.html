--- category: grains question: "Why do you say that pizza is especially bad? – it is just a kind of bread with some vegetables and anchovies on it." source: "September 2001" ---

First of all, pizza is more than that –it usually has a lot of melted cheese too. Many people add pepperoni, Bologna and other artery-rotting meats. All this makes for a food-combining nightmare. But that is not the main point. Studies have shown that pizza has an exaggerated hyperglycemic effect. Instead of glucose levels dropping down to zero after about four hours as might be expected, they increase for another five hours! This graphically demonstrates how treacherous it is to doubleguess the Glycemic Index of a food. It has to be measured directly. Pizza is assuredly one of the most unhealthy, obesity-making foods that you can put in you body.
