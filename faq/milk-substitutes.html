--- category: dairy question: "If I do eat some cereal, would rice milk, soymilk or another milk be the better addition? (I'm thinking about the problem of mixing carbohydrates and protein.)" source: "September 2002" ---

The purist would not dream of eating cereals and so would not be faced with the dilemma as to what liquid to put on them. However, if you must indulge, rice milk and soymilk are much better alternatives than mammary-gland milk of any kind. Do watch out for the added sugars! These substitute milks are mostly water and the tiny amount of protein in soymilk is of no consequence at all.
